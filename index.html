The Value Landscape

How a network is grown, not written: a random guess, a score, one small step uphill, repeated. Then two hundred and forty climbers settle on different hills, a flaw in the scoring turns out to be high ground too, and one shared value draws the many together, but only once they are free to wander.

Zero-Intelligence Market

The Dyad Lab

Gottman and Murray's marriage equations, drawn — two null-clines, their steady states, and the watershed between them. Then four hundred couples at once, where 94% looking back becomes 29% looking forward. Set every couple identical and the gap is still there.
